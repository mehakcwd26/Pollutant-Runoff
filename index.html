Campus Drainage & Pollution Sources

Four key ways rainwater picks up pollution as it moves across campus.

🔬

Surface Microplastics

Tire wear, shredding synthetic turf, and degraded packaging break into tiny particles. Rain washes them into drains where they are very hard to remove.

🍽️

Canteen Wastewater & Organic Runoff

Food scraps, oils, and detergents rinsed outdoors enter drains and use up oxygen in water, harming aquatic life.

🚗

Parking Lot Hydrocarbons & Heavy Metals

Leaking oil, fuel, and brake dust leave residues on pavement. Stormwater carries them downstream as toxic pollutants.

🌊

Storm Drain Pathways & Water Body Impact

Most storm drains are untreated and lead straight to nearby ponds, canals, or rivers, so campus pollution becomes local water pollution.

Recommendations for University Administration

Four practical steps to reduce stormwater pollution.

  • Build bio-retention swales and rain gardens to slow and filter runoff.
  • Replace single-use plastics near outdoor seating with reusable or compostable options.
  • Clean drains regularly, especially before monsoon season.
  • Install fine-mesh drain filters to catch litter and larger microplastics.
